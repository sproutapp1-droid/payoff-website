# SEO content plan: 50 new articles

Date: 29 July 2026

## Evidence used

- Google Search Console performance export for 28 April to 27 July 2026
- Google Search Console page indexing export for 30 April to 24 July 2026
- Existing article, guide, calculator, sitemap, metadata, and internal-link inventory
- Current search results for debt payoff planner, debt payoff app, calculator, tracker, and credit card payoff topics

## What the data says

- The site earned 1,606 impressions and 11 clicks in the exported period.
- "Debt payoff planner" led with 274 impressions, followed by "debt payoff planner app" with 137.
- Closely related app and planner terms account for most of the visible demand.
- The strongest existing article positions are for student loan payoff strategies, extra payments, the debt payoff tracker, and how to pay off credit card debt.
- Only 22 pages were indexed while 260 were not indexed at the end of the coverage export.
- Google reported 194 pages as discovered but not indexed and 34 as crawled but not indexed.
- Both `www` and non-`www` URLs appear in performance data, which indicates canonical fragmentation.
- Existing article links to `/en/blog/...` return 404 because the blog lives at `/blog/...`.

## Content architecture

The blog will use seven crawlable categories:

1. Apps and Tools
2. Calculators
3. Credit Card Debt
4. Payoff Strategies
5. Budgeting and Habits
6. Loans and Life Events
7. Motivation and Wellbeing

Each new article has one primary search intent, links to its category hub, links to a relevant calculator, and links to two or three related articles. Existing generic categories such as "Guides" and "Features" will be consolidated into these clearer topic clusters.

## Editorial rules

- Answer the primary question directly near the top.
- Use plain English, short sections, descriptive headings, worked examples, and an action checklist.
- Do not use em dashes.
- Do not invent statistics, testimonials, professional credentials, ratings, or outcomes.
- Cite primary sources for facts that can change or materially affect a financial decision.
- Distinguish general education from individual financial advice.
- Include US and UK context only where it is accurate and useful.
- Avoid creating a new page when the intent is already owned by an existing article.

## Cannibalisation boundaries

- The existing "How to Pay Off Credit Card Debt Fast" page remains the broad credit card hub. New card articles answer narrower questions and link back to it.
- The existing calculator guide remains the broad calculator hub. New calculator articles target a specific input or result.
- The existing tracker guide owns "debt payoff tracker." New app articles focus on platform, privacy, setup, or format.
- The existing snowball and avalanche guides own the broad strategy terms. New articles apply those methods specifically to multiple credit cards.
- The existing irregular-income article remains the authority for freelance and variable income. The low-income and one-income articles address different constraints.

## Recommended release order

1. Repair canonical and internal-link issues and launch category hubs.
2. Publish P1 articles in small, internally linked groups.
3. Add P2 articles after each relevant cluster has a strong hub and calculator path.
4. Request indexing for category hubs and the strongest P1 pages, not every URL at once.
5. Review Search Console after four to six weeks and update titles, introductions, and internal links based on query data.
